## What is SW4P?

SW4P is an intelligent routing and settlement system for moving value across the internet.

A payment, transfer or exchange starts with an intended outcome. SW4P selects a route and coordinates the authorisation, fees, gas and execution needed to carry it through settlement. It tracks what completed, what failed and what requires recovery.

These capabilities are programmable and composable. Software can call them directly and combine them across multiple steps, making value movement part of how an application operates.

## Value at the speed of thought

SW4P’s vision is to make moving value as natural as expressing intent. The ambition is dependable completion with less effort, cost and delay, wherever value needs to move.

## How SW4P Works

### 1. Define the operation

A request identifies the asset and amount to move, the recipient and the intended result. It includes the conditions under which the operation can proceed.

### 2. Resolve the route

SW4P identifies a path across the supported assets and networks, along with the authorisation, fees and gas needed to use it.

### 3. Execute and track

The operation follows that path through the required transactions and confirmations.

### 4. Account for the result

The result identifies what settled and what remains unresolved, including any work required for recovery.
